---
title: Awarding Merits
version: 2
---
Merits are used to measure how well the group did in their mission. They are a measure of renown and prestige within their faction. Each Mission Objective is worth a certain amount of Merits. 

- **Primary Objectives**: Critical to mission completion. 10 Merits on success
- **Secondary Objectives**: Important objectives, but maybe not critical to mission success. 5 Merits on success.
- **Optional Objectives**: Bonus objectives that are completely optional. 1 Merits each.

*Each member* of the team generally receives the same amount of merits. Commonwealth does not usually consider individual performance, but other Factions may have different ideas.

For long-running campaigns, you might want to break down the mission into smaller pieces, where each part of the mission can earn separate merits. Additionally, for high-stakes, you can multiply the received merits by suitable multiplier, such as x2 or x4 – any more than that would likely be too much.

Characters should keep track of received merits on their character sheet. Once they have gathered enough merits, they may be awarded **promotion**, improving their [[chargen-faction|Faction Rank]]. A badly failed mission may even result in *demerits*, and facing a possible demotion.
