---
title: Awarding Experience
---
As you play stories the characters will progress in their abilities. This progression is reflected by **Experience Points** `(XP)`, which are earned after each session. Character must keep track of their earned experience points, but they must wait until downtime to actually be able to [[experience|spend any experience points]].

Each game session should end with a short retrospective on how the session went. Have a free discussion, gather feedback and finally award experience points. GM is free to decide how quickly they want their players to progress, but generally speaking, characters should earn at least 1 XP per 2-4h session, but on average characters should gain 2-3 XP per game session.

**Session Experience**

- **Session Participation**: Everyone  gets 1 XP for participating the session
- **Danger**: If you were in serious danger, you get 1 XP
- **Intrigue**: Participating in high stakes intrigue is worth 1 XP
- **Humanity**: Character expressed their human side, taking into account their Engrams. Worth at least 1 XP.
- **Creativity:** Thinking outside the box, keeping everyone entertained. Worth 1 XP.
- **Mission:** Taking actions to proceed with shared objectives earns 1 XP. Mission Objectives mainly affect how many [[gm-merits|Merits]] are earned, but a little bit of XP should be awarded as well.

Some things that should not be awarded:

- Random Violence
- Deliberately hindering or complicating team efforts, unless there is a solid cause.
- Taking all the spotlight. Everyone should have a turn to play.
