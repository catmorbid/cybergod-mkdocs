---
title: Multitasking
version: 4
---
Multitasking means trying to do multiple things at the same time. The more you try to accomplish, the more difficult it is.

Examples of multitasking
- *”I run to cover and shoot while running”*
- *”I want to use Breach on the vault door while uploading a Reboot hack on the combat drone, and use my scout drone to track the enemy hacker”*
- *”I want to distract the bouncer by flirting with him, while pickpocketing the credit qube I just saw him pocket*

You can attempt as many simultaneous tasks you want, but you must succeed with less dice than usual:
- Subtract a number of dice equal to total *number of simultaneous actions*, from each *dice pool*.
- Subtract modifier dice first, then human dice
- You cannot perform the same action twice in a row.
- The multitasking sequences should be logical, practical and possible. Use reason and common sense.

If an action does not require test, but *does require your attention*, you still count it for determining dice reduction. If any dice pool is reduced to zero, you cannot succeed in the test while multitasking. Actions that do not require a test are unaffected and will always succeed.

The dice reduction is affected by a **Multitasking Modifier** determined by your [[attributes#Mind|Mind]] attribute. A *dull* Mind means an additional die is subtracted, but a *focused* Mind rating or better means you get less penalty. No matter how good your *mind* attribute, you cannot *gain* bonus dice from **Multitasking Modifier**.
