---
title: "Complication: Fail"
version: 1
---

A **Fail** **Complication** is the most common (and most boring) type of complication, which can be applied to any test. It simply tells you that unless overcome, the test will fail. **Fail Complication** is typically inflicted by [[systems/conditions/index|Conditions]] such as [[dazed]] or [[damage-system-v4#Pain Level|Pain Level]].

Fail condition *does not stack*, instead you use the highest source. E.g. If you have *Fail 3* due to [[damage-system-v4#Pain Level|Pain Level]], and *Fail 1* due to [[dazed]], you only use the highest, i.e. *Fail 3*.
