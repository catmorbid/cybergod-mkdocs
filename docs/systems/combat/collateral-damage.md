Collateral damage represents unwanted damage beyond the attacker’s control towards surroundings, allies, innocent bystanders or additional enemies. Collateral Damage can be represented in two different ways:

- **Collateral Targets:** You must still split the total hits between all your targets, including collateral targets, resulting in less damage to the primary target.
- **Collateral Hits:** A single Hit that must hit a collateral target, but doesn’t affect how normal Hits are distributed.
- **[[weapon-stats-v4#Area|Area of Effect]]:** Defines an area. Everyone and everything is a target in the area.

Collateral Damage always hits *something*: If a valid target – enemy, friendly or innocent bystander – is not present, then collateral targets simply destroys something in the surroundings, such as windows, lamps, electronics, bottles, valuable paintings, furniture and so on.
