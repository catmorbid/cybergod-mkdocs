---
title: Pain Level
version: 1
---

Sum the total **Pain Level** added by [[conditions/index|Conditions]] and [[damage-conditions|Damage]]. Apply a [[penalties|Penalty]] equal to **Pain Level** to any *Body*, *Reflex* or *Mind* tests (but not Cyber tests).

**Pain Mitigation** is reduced from current **Pain Level** before applying the penalty and the penalty can be entirely nullified.

Every character has a [[stats#Pain Threshold|Pain Threshold]], which works as a natural Pain mitigation. Additional Pain mitigation can be obtained through *Medicine*, *Drugs* and *Augments*.

!!! example
	Cyber Joe has taken 2 Harm and 1 Trauma after engaging two combat mechs. His Pain Level is 3, which is still below his *Pain Threshold* of 4, so he does not take any penalties yet.
