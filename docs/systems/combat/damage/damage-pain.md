---
title: Pain Level
version: 1
---

**Pain Level** is determined by total [[damage-harm|Harm]] taken. Pain Level is applied as [[penalties|Penalty]] to any *Body*, *Reflex* or *Mind* tests (but not Cyber tests). *Maximum Pain Level* is 10. Any further harm taken does not add to Pain.

 [[stats#Pain Threshold|Pain Threshold]] is subtracted from current **Pain Level**. Any Pain equal or less than *Pain Threshold* can be simply ignored. Every character has natural *Pain Threshold*, which can be augmented through *Medicine*, *Drugs* and *Augments*.

At **Pain Level 5** or greater, the character is automatically [[incapacitated]].

!!! example
	Cyber Joe has taken 3 harm after engaging two combat mechs. His Pain Level is 3, which is still below his *Pain Threshold* of 4, so he does not take any penalties yet.
