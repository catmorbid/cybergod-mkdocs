Lasting damage and injuries can be healed *naturally* with enough time, but recovery time can be significantly improved by applying *Medical care* or *Repairs*. Certain [[character-creation/augments/_old/index|Augments]] may help [[regenerating|regenerate]] damage and advanced medical consumables can help as well.


| Damage           | Natural Recovery                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Shock            | [[recovery-test\|Recovery Test]] during [[turns#Upkeep Phase\|Upkeep]] phase; full recovery at end of scene. |
| Minor Harm       | 1 point at end of scene.                                                                                     |
| Serious Harm     | 1 point at end of day.                                                                                       |
| Devastating Harm | 1 point at end of week                                                                                       |
| Fatal Harm       | 1 point at end of month.                                                                                     |

## Field Medic Test
Test `MedTech (Mind)` to administer emergency treatment in combat conditions.

A single Field Medic treatment takes 1 full round. The cost for for healing 1 point of harm varies according to highest Harm Level. You can treat a character once for their wounds, unless they take additional harm.

You need a **Field Medical Kit**, which includes the necessary tools and drugs. Inferior utilities may be helpful as well, but the Effect Point cost of successful treatment should be increased.

| Damage           | Effect Point Cost |
| ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Shock            | 1 per 5 Shock     |
| Minor Harm       | 1                 |
| Serious Harm     | 2                 |
| Devastating Harm | 3                 |
| Fatal Harm       | 4                 |

## Long-term recovery
Severe injuries take a long time to recover naturally. With proper treatment and advanced medicine, the recovery time can be increased significantly.

Cyborgs do not generally need to worry about natural healing as long as they have access to their Faction’s resources; they can simply change their Frame while the original is healing. Factions utilize Frame Templates to be effective; it is easier to work with a common baseline to manufacture a new frame in few hours instead of waiting weeks or months for a Frame to regenerate naturally. In worst cases, a Frame is simply recycled for biomass and rare components.
