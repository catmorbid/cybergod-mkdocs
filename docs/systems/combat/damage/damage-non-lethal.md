title: Non-Lethal Damage
version: 4
tags:

---
Half of damage, *before* applying [[damage-soak|Soak]] is applied as [[damage-shock|Shock]]. Leftover Soak can reduce Shock as well.

> !!! example
> 	Joe is hit by a stun gun, dealing non-lethal damage. Joe takes 2 hits of DC 6 damage from a single hit, reducing it to 10 via DR and then rolls total 6 soak, reducing damage to 4. Half of that is inflicted as Shock, so Joe takes total 8 Shock and 2 Harm.
