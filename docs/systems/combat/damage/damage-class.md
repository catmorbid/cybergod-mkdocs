title: Damage Class
version: 4
---

**Damage Class (DC)** is a classification system for a weapon’s power, indicated by a numeric value.

When target is hit in combat, *Damage Class* equals to **Weapon Damage Class** + **1 per extra Hit** on target. Apply [[damage-soak|Soak]] to mitigate damage. Remaining damage then inflicts [[damage-harm|Harm]].
