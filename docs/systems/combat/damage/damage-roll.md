---
title: Damage Roll
version: 4
---
**Damage Roll** is used to determine how much damage and *attack*, or a *hazard* inflicts. Damage Roll in its most simple form can be any combination of dice, such as `4d10+4d8`. For typical attacks, roll your **Damage Dice** derived from *effective* [[damage-class|Damage Class]] *per Hit*.

!!! example
	*July* is making a damage roll. She has effective **Damage Class** of 7 and scored 4 Hits on her target. **Damage Dice** from `DC 7` is `D8+D6`, so her total Damage Roll is `4D8+4D6`.

- Roll all relevant dice at once
- Accumulate sum of rolled values
- The total is the damage inflicted, which is used to determine [[damage-conditions|Damage Conditions]]
