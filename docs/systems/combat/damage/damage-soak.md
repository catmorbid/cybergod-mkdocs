---
title: Soak Test
---


Test [[stats#Damage Threshold]], and for any effects obtained, you can convert 1 damage from an attack’s total damage to [[damage-shock|1 Shock]]. This reduces the [[damage-harm|Harm]] taken, but will eventually impair the character.

Certain augments or equipment can provide bonuses to **Soak** rating. Soak Rating is unaffected by [[damage-shred|Shred]] effects.

example “Armor layers”

> Marcus has level 2 Dermal Armor augment, which provides 2D6 Soak. On top of that he’s wearing a Combat Armor which provide 2 Armor. His Toughness + Resilient is 4 and his Body is D10, so he rolls 4D10+2d6 soak test and can mitigate additional 2 points of damage on top of that.

example “Joe chasing part 2”

> Joe score a single critical Hit on his target and inflicts DC 8 damage with heavy sniper weapon. But any penetrating damage (not shock though) is *tripled* if any damage goes through soak. The target has 4D8 Soak Dice and armor with 2 hard mitigation, reducing damage do 6. They then roll Soak and gain 4 soft mitigation, reducing damage to 2, but also adding 4 shock. The remaining 2 damage is *tripled* for total 6 damage. The target takes *devastating harm* and collapses due to pain, but remains alive regardless.
