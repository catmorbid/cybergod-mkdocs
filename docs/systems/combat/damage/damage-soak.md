---
title: Soak Test
---

Test `[Toughness]d[Body]`, and apply any Effect Points as **Soft Mitigation**.

**Intrinsic Armor** can be provide by certain augments, and it can add **Modifier Dice** to Soak test. Intrinsic armor cannot be be **Shred**.

example “Armor layers”

> Marcus has level 2 Dermal Armor augment, which provides 2D6 intrinsic armor. On top of that he’s wearing a combat Armor with 2 Soft mitigation and 2 Hard mitigation. His Toughness+Resilient is 4 and his Body is D10, so he rolls 4D10+2d6 soak test and can mitigate a total of 6 points of damage on top of that.

example “Joe chasing part 2”

> Joe score a critical Hit on his target and inflicts DC 8 damage. But any penetrating damage (not shock though) is *tripled* if any damage goes through soak. The target has has 4D8 Soak Dice and armor with 2 hard mitigation, reducing damage do 6. They then roll Soak and gain 4 EP, reducing damage to 2 with 4 soft mitigation, but also adding 4 shock. The remaining 2 damage is *tripled* for total 6 damage. The target takes *devastating harm* and collapses due to pain, but remains alive regardless.
