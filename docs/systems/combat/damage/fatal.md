tags:

If you’re out of Trauma Slots or a single attack inflicts equal or more damage than your [[stats#Damage Threshold|Fatal Threshold]], you take a Fatal condition, representing severe, life-threatening injuries and critical structural damage. You will die unless the damage is immediately treated. If survived the character is [[collapsed|Collapsed]] and are at [[damage-pain|+3 Pain Level]] until recovered.
