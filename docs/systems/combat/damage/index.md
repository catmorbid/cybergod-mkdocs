---
title: Damage Conditions
version: 1
---
Damage Conditions determine the severity of structural damage, wounds or injuries inflicted. When [[damage-system-v4|Taking Damage]], you check mark the highest damage condition resulting from the rolled damage, compared to your [[stats#Damage Threshold|Damage Threshold]].

There are 5 possible damage conditions, from least to most harmful:
- [[shock]]
- [[harm]]
- [[trauma]]
- [[fatal]]
- [[overkill]]
