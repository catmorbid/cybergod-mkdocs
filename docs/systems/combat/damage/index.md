Resolve Damage by following the procedure laid out in these steps:

1. Determine number of *Hits* generated by *Effects*.
2. Determine [[damage-class|Damage Class (DC)]]: Base DC +1 per extra hit.
3. Apply [[damage-mitigation|Damage Mitigation]] and make [[damage-soak|Soak Test]].
4. Apply [[damage-harm|Harm]]
5. Note any additional [[conditions/index|Conditions]]


!!! Example
	A street punk pulls out a light SMG and fires on full auto at Cyber Joe, obtaining 4 Hits at DC 3, adding +3 DC from extra hits for total 6 Damage. Cyber Joe has 2 hard mitigation and rolls 4 soak, and doesn’t take any harm, only 4 shock
	
	Cyber Joe retaliates immediately, charging into close combat and delivering a powerful cybernetically augmented attack for 2 hits at DC 9 for total 10 damage. The punk only rolls 1 soak and takes 9 Harm for devastating damage.
