title: Damage System

---
Resolve Damage by following the procedure laid out in these steps:

1. Determine number of *Hits* generated by *Effects*.
2. Determine **Damage Per Hit** and apply [[damage-armor|Armor]]: $AttackDC-Armor$
3. Determine **Total Damage:** $DamagePerHit*Hits$
4. Make a [[damage-soak|Soak Test]] to mitigate any damage left, converting damage to Shock instead.
5. Apply [[damage-harm|Harm]] and any [[systems/conditions/index|Conditions]]
6. Check [[damage-pain|Pain Level]]

!!! example
	A street punk pulls out a light SMG and fires on full auto at Cyber Joe, obtaining 4 Hits at DC 3, adding +3 DC from extra hits for total 6 Damage. Cyber Joe has 2 hard mitigation and rolls 4 soak, and doesn’t take any harm, only 4 shock
	
	Cyber Joe retaliates immediately, charging into close combat and delivering a powerful cybernetically augmented attack DC9 and gains 2 hits, increasing DC to 10. The punk only rolls 1 soak and has 1 soft mitigation from armor and takes 8 Harm. Being drugged they have Pain Threshold 3, but that still leaves them with pain level 5, incapacitating them outright.
