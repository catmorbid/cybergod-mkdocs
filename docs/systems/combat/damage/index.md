title: Damage System
version: 4.2
---
Resolve Damage by following the procedure laid out in these steps:

1. Determine number of *Hits* generated by *Effects*.
2. Determine **Damage Per Hit** and apply [[damage-armor|Armor]]: $AttackDC-ArmorDR$
3. Determine **Total Damage:** $DamagePerHit*Hits$
4. Multiply Damage by **Damage Multipliers** – if any
5. Make a [[damage-soak|Soak Test]] to mitigate any damage left, converting damage to Shock instead.
6. Apply [[damage-harm|Harm]] and any [[conditions/index|Conditions]]
7. Check if you are [[incapacitated]]:
	1. [[damage-pain|Pain Level 5+]]
	2. [[damage-shock|Full Shock]]

!!! example
	A street punk pulls out a light SMG with ROF 3 and fires on full auto at Cyber Joe, obtaining maximum i.e. 3 Hits at DC 3. Joe has Body D10 and Toughness 4 and is wearing Dermal Armor 1 and Ballistic Weave for total DR 1 and Soak 5d10+2d6. DC of the attack is reduced to DC 2, multiplied by 3 hits for total 6 damage. Joe rolls soak 6 soak, mitigating all damage, but takes 6 Shock.
	
	Cyber Joe retaliates immediately, charging into close combat and delivering a powerful cybernetically augmented strike with base DC 4, 2 hits and +3 power for 2xDC 7 damage. The punk has 1 DR and rolls 4 soak for (12-4) 8 lethal damage and 4 shock and Pain Level 8. Being drugged they have Pain Threshold 3, but that still leaves them with pain level 5, incapacitating them outright.
