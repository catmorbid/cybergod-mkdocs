---
title: Instant Actions
version: 1
---

**Instant** actions can be taken without consuming an actual action or reaction. Anything that takes merely *a fraction of a second* counts as an Instant Action.

Note that if you combine enough instant actions to one, then they may surely take a full Action instead. Examples of *Instant* actions include:

- Drop an item
- Speak a few words
- Activate an *augment* that can be activated *in an instant*.
- Quickly drop to prone stance
- Assign a quick command to remote-operated drone
- Prepare a grenade or item readily available (e.g. on utility belt etc.)
