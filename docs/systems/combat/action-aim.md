tags:

---
When you take an Aim action, you take time to aim a subsequent [[ranged-combat-v4|Ranged Attack]], giving the following benefits:

- Add the weapon’s **CTRL** dice as modifier on the attack test if **Rate of Fire** is either *Single*, *Semi* or *Burst*.
- Add +2 Initiative when resolving a [[conflicting-test|Conflicting Test]]
