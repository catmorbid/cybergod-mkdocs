title: Death Test
version: 1
---
**System**
Test `Toughness (Body)`. The cost to succeed the test is equal to the amount of [[damage-harm|Fatal Harm]], i.e. Harm marked at Fatal slots. If you succeed exceedingly well, your wounds stabilize and you are no longer [[dying]] unless you take further Harm.


| Effect   | Cost    | Description                                                                |
| -------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Survive  | [Fatal] | You survive for another round. Repeat Death Test during next upkeep phase  |
| Stablize | 5       | You are stabilized and no longer Dying, unless you take another Fatal hit. |
