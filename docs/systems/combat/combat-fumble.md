---
title: Fumbles in Combat
ver: "1"
---
A **Fumble** represents a  *critical failure*, a *royal screw-up*, or simply a *massive mangle of mess* that absolutely will ruin your day. Luckily they are not very common, in fact normal tests cannot result in *Fumbles* at all. However, certain *complications* may call for a fumble, unless overcome. GM should present that a complication can result in a fumble before you make the test, giving you some time to reconsider.

Fumbles are only used in combat, normally GM can present any *consequence* for standard tests. But since combat is chaotic and unpredictable, it is hard to estimate all consequences accurately.

Resolve Fumbles by rolling on the appropriate table.

!!! tip "Adjustable Fumble Roll"
	Fumble rolls are completely optional, although they can make combat more interesting and chaotic. The GM can decide the size of the die used on the roll between D4 and D12, indicating how big of screw-up it can be – or you can just universally agree to always use a specific die, in order to avoid too drastic results. GM can opt to not use dice to at all, and instead simply decide the outcome every time – or you can ignore fumbles entirely and just treat it as a simple failure.

| Roll | Fumble Result                                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Butterfingers** – You succeed in the attack but **drop your weapon**. Resolve damage normally, then spend an action to retrieve or switch weapons. |
| 2    | **Structural Failure** – You succeed in the attack but **break your weapon**. Resolve damage normally; the weapon is now unusable.                   |
| 3    | **Whiff** – You simply **miss the attack**. No further effect.                                                                                       |
| 4    | **Slip-Up** – You miss the attack and **lose your next action** (or Reaction). You overextend or stumble badly.                                      |
| 5    | **Collateral Damage** – You hit a **nearby ally or civilian**. They may test Alertness + Mind to react. Resolve damage normally.                     |
| 6    | **Jam or Lock** – You miss, and your weapon **jams or locks**. You must spend an action to clear or reset it. Melee weapons are considered stuck.    |
| 7    | **Weapon Break** – You miss and your weapon **breaks catastrophically**. Beyond repair without time, tools, and a suitable skill test.               |
| 8    | **Overexertion** – Your body rebels and you’re about to give up in desperation. Consume 1 Energy and take 1 Stress.                                  |
| 9    | **Critical Exposure** – You expose yourself to **enemy retaliation**. The next enemy to act against you gains **d10 advantage** against you.         |
| 10   | **Self-Inflicted** – You fumble so hard you **hit yourself**. Roll and resolve **normal damage** against yourself.                                   |
| 11   | **Chain Reaction** – You trigger an **environmental hazard** nearby (e.g., explosive canister, volatile equipment). GM decides consequence.          |
| 12   | **Explosive Malfunction** – Your weapon **explodes/shatters** in your hand. Take **3 × DC 6** damage. Adjacent allies may suffer **DC 4** damage.    |
