---
title: Multiple Targets
version: 1
---
You can choose to split multiple Hits between primary target and additional targets. This split is usually possible whenever you have more than one hit. Hits must be distributed as evenly as possible between each additional target. As each target may have their own armor and soak values, you resolve the damage for each target separately, after determining the number of hits per target.

!!! Example
	Mick Graves is operating a machinegun and providing fire support for his team. He targets a group of 3 enemies, and scores 5 hits. Two enemies get 2 hits and third gets 1 hit.
