---
title: Recovery Test
version: 1
---

During upkeep phase, you may make a Recovery Test. Normally you can heal Shock and removed Stunned condition, but when augments are activated it becomes possible to recover from more serious damage and conditions.

**System**

Test `Mind (Toughness)` and spend Effect Points according to the following table:

| Effect          | Cost | Description                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recover Shock   | 1R   | When **not** [[stunned\|Stunned]]. Repeatable. Recover 1 [[damage-shock]] condition.                                                                                                                      |
| Recover Stunned | 3    | When [[stunned\|Stunned]]. Remove Stunned Condition and recover 1 Shock.                                                                                                                                  |
| Tolerate Pain   | 2R   | Repeatable. Ignore 1 level of **Pain**.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Fight Through   | 4    | When [[incapacitated]], ignore the condition but act as if [[stunned]]. Remain active for one more turn. The states persist even on success and if you fail, you’re out for good and unable to try again. |

*Recovery Test Effects table* `R` = repeatable
