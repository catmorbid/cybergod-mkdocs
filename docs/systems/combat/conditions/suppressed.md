---
title: Suppressed
tags:
  - Damage
  - Condition
version: 1
---

Target is under fire by opposition. Hits are not landing straight, but there is a chance to get hit unless they stick to cover.

Target has -1 penalty on any actions while suppressed. For each action other than sticking to cover or laying low, they have 1 in 10 chance to get a hit from a stray bullet or shot.
