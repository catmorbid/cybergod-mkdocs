tags:

---
A stunned character cannot declare or perform an action. They can still make their **Reaction**, but take [[skill-test#Complication Fail|Fail 2 Complication]] on the test.

You can recover from Stunned condition by making [[recovery-test|Recovery Test]] during [[turns#Upkeep Phase|Upkeep]] phase.

You can ignore *stunned* condition for 1 round by spending a point of [[resolve|Resolve]].
