title: Incapacitated
version: 1
tags:

---
You are incapacitated and cannot take any actions. You have one chance during next [[turns#Upkeep Phase|Upkeep phase]] to *fight through* for one more round. If you fail, you are taken out of combat. You can be **revived** with suitable drugs, such as a large dose of adrenaline.
