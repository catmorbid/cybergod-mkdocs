---
title: Incapacitated
version: 1
tags:

  - Condition
---
You are [[stunned]] until end of current round. You have one chance during next [[turns#Upkeep Phase|Upkeep phase]] to *fight through*. If you fail, you are taken out of combat. You can be **revived** with suitable drugs, such as a large dose of adrenaline, or certain *augments*.
