If a single attack inflicts more damage than your [[stats#Damage Threshold|Trauma Threshold]], or you’re out of Harm slots, you take one point of Trauma. The character is [[incapacitated|Incapacitated]] and takes [[damage-#Pain Level|+2 Pain Level]], if they manage to remain functional.
