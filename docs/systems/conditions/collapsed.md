title: Collapsed
version: 1
tags:
  - Condition
---
You have suffered massive physical shock and as a consequence will fall unconscious. You have one chance to *fight through* during next [[turns#Upkeep Phase|Upkeep]], but if you fail, you’re taken out of combat. You *cannot be revived* during combat, but may be revived next scene as long as you’re given some medical aid, otherwise you’re permanently incapacitated, until GM’s discretion.
