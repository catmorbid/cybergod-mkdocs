---
title: Cyber Warfare
---
Cyber Warfare is an umbrella term that covers acts like Electronic Warfare, system breach and data theft, uploading malicious viruses in combat to hinder or harm your opponents, or even hacking a cyborg’s cybernetic brain.

- [[cyberwarfare-suite|Cyberwarfare Suite]] is an augment designed to enable powerful **Combat Hacks**
- [[cyberjacking-suite|Cyberjacking Suite]] is an augment for attacking the cybernetic brain of a cyborg
- [[disruptor-suite|Disruptor Suite]] is a dedicated augment for Electronic Warfare and Anti-Cyborg interference and disruption

While the above cover specific circumstances, the most generic cyber warfare actions are covered by [[system-breach|System Breach]] rules, which can be executed without specialized augments, although you do need specialized software. System Breach specialists may wish to install the [[system-breach-suite|System Breach Suite]]
