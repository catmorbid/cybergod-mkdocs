---
title: Cyber Damage
---
Cyber weapons are capable of inflicting damage on afflicted targets.
- **Biofeedback:** feedback pulse directed at neural connection that can cause permanent nerve damage and even death. Biofeedback inflicts **Harm**.
- **Interface Shock:** Mental and physical stress caused by unstable connection and disturbing stimuli. Interface Shock inflicts **Shock** and is not lethal.

You can resist Cyber Damage by making a `Troubleshooting (Cyber)` test. Each Effect Point resists one point of damage.
