title: Psionic Resonance
tags:
  - Psionics
---
- **Cognitive Resonance** – thoughts and memories, either emanating strong with people around; or faint lingering presence, almost like memories that stain a physical place
- **Kinetic Resonance** – physical forces, friction, energy
- **Chrono-Spatial Resonance** – Resonance that connects places through space-time, allows glimpsing at *alternate realities*, the *future* and the *past* or simply connecting to a *remote* place.
