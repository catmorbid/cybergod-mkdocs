---
title: Concentration
tags:
  - Psionics
---
PSI-user can spend up to *three consecutive* **Actions** on *Concentrating* before making the [[psionics-test|PSI Test]]. Each action spent grants an additional [[augment-effect-advantage|Augment Advantage Die]]. 

Spending 2-3 actions on concentration means the actual [[psionics-test|PSI Test]] is made on the following round.
- **Interruptions:** If you take damage or are affected by any kind of mentally or physically disorienting effect, you need to make a `Moderate(2) Toughness + Mind` Test to maintain concentration. If you fail, you lose all concentration gained so far.
