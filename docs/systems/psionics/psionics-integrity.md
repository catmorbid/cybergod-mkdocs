[[systems/psionics/index|Psionics]] uses a unique resources called **“PSI Integrity”**, which starts at **D10**. *PSI Integrity* determines how stable your relation is to the **PSI-Net**, and the lower it is, the more likely it is for you to generate [[psionics-echoes|Psionic Echoes]]. PSI Integrity may be consumed by using high-level augments, in which case you need to make a standard Resource Check, and on failure, you must roll on the [[psionics-echoes|Psionic Echoes table]] as well as degrade the resource by one step. PSI Integrity improves naturally over time, as long as the user refrains from consuming any PSI Integrity.
	- **D4** → **D6** in 8 hours
	- **D6** → **D8** in 48 hours
	- **D8** → **D10** in 2 weeks
	- **D10** → **D12** in 3 months

If PSI-integrity degrades to zero (degrade from D4), any use of psionics will result in a [[psionics-echoes|Psionic Echo]], and if you use them more than once during a scene, you need to roll two dice – GM picks the result.
