---
title: Effect Combinations
tags:
  - Psionics
---

You can combine any available Effects in a single **PSI-Test**, as long as you have enough **Effect Points** available, and the combination makes some sense (GM fiat). E.g. You can combine *Kinetic Force Impulse* with *Thermokinesis* to create a powerful force impulse that lights things on fire; or *Scan* and *Illusion* to read someone’s mind while playing out an illusion – perhaps as a clever way to provoke a certain thought.
