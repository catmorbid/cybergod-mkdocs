# The Radical X

Since the early adoption of mainstream AI, voices arose to defend art as human domain, dismissing AI-generated arts as destructive to humankind. When Commonwealth was established, the resistance was strong as the notion of Super Intelligent AI’s *ruling* over humankind was unfathomable. *The Radical Human Expression Front* has its roots in the resistance movements from those early days of Commonwealth, who started out as an opinionated group of artists and free thinkers that resisted the use of AI in aspects of society that should be clearly human dominion, such as entertainment, music, arts and government. They were always highly critical of the Commonwealth, but while they were known for being fervent defenders of freedom of speech and expression, they were never violent. They were organized and effectively held rallies, protests and public art exhibitions, some of which were borderline vandalism. Not soon after the *Bloody New Year* they radicalized rapidly and earned high influx of new members traumatized by the massacre. They soon took a hard pivot into more *radical* methods of voicing their opinion, taking cues from many terrorist organizations from human history.

*The Radical X* of today have evolved into full-blown terrorists, operating in multiple independent cells throughout the world, with serious financial backing behind them. Their objective is to take down the Commonwealth by any means necessary, including mass murder, suicide bombings, sabotage, assassination. Their true leadership is unknown, kept secret by effective cell structure, but some conspiracies claim they're working for someone high up in the Commonwealth.

The Radical X fights against the Commonwealth and anything that has to do with it. They occasionally work with the *Syndicate* and the *Order of the Void*, and some conspiracies claim agents of the clandestine *Section 12* sometimes use them as tools in their own war.

!!! info "The Crucible"
	Radical X cyborgs can be fitted with dangerous Energy Core modification, they dubbed *”The Crucible”* – their well-known weapon of mass destruction and terror. The Crucible is effectively a self-destruction device they use spread terror. Those who carry the devices are often referred as “Martyrs”. In reality, their sacrifice is meaningless one, as they are often backed up remotely and resurrect soon after their deed is done.
