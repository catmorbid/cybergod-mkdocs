---
title: Frame
---
Frame is the physical body that character uses as host for their consciousness. Consciousness is digital and if you die you can be *reborn* in another frame. You pick a [[frame-template-v4|Frame Template]] from those provided by your [[factions/index|Faction]], but as you gain ranks in your chosen Faction, you receive [[upgrades]] that can be spent on modifying, improving and customizing your frame.
