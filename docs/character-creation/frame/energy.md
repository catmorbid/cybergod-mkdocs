---
title: Energy
---


**Energy** is used to fuel Augments. Each **Frame** starts with **Energy Level** of **D4** and should be quickly upgraded. *Energy* is a [[character-creation/resources/index|Resource]], and when spending energy, mark down one slot to track usage, then make a normal [[resource-check|Resource Check]]. If you fail, you take a point of [[burn]], but the usage track remains.

All **Spent** energy spend resets during [[turns#Upkeep Phase|Upkeep Phase]] of combat and *end of scene*, but you cannot clear it below current [[burn|Burn Level]].

!!! example
	Boris burned a lot of energy during a tough fight and now has 2 points of Burn. At the end of the turn he has used 5 points of energy. A new round starts, and Boris clears spent energy to 2 points.
