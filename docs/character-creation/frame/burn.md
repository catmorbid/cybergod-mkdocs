---
title: Burn
---

Burn represents long-term, potentially hazardous strain on the entire system that can harm the frame or cause system shutdown.

Whenever you take a point of **Burn**, mark it down and make an [[resource-check|Energy Resource Check]] with current Burn: On failure, the frame shuts down for a **short duration** and the character is [[stunned]].

You can force the frame to push through the shutdown, but this will inflict [[harm]].

**Burn** is very slow to recover and passive it is cleared by **one point** at the *end of a scene*. A regular maintenance tune-up requiring some tools, materials and a **Moderate Duration**. Test `Moderate(2) Troubleshooting + Mind` to clear all accumulated *Burn*.
