---
title: Upgrades
version: 4
---

As the character improves their **Faction Rank**, they will receive **Upgrades**, represented by increase in **Frame Level**. For each Frame Level after the first, the character receives the following benefits:
- 2 Augment Levels
- 1 Attribute Level or 1 Energy Level

## Upgrade Path

You can choose to follow a pre-determined upgrade Path for a Frame Template. In this case, all you need to do is consult the **Frame Level** table for each template to determine attributes, energy level and augments.
