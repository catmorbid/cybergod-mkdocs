---
title: Frame Technology
---
Frame Technology determines how the frame is built and may affect some mechanics. The possible Frame Technologies are **Biotech**, **Cybertech** and **Nanotech**.

## Biotech

- **Soak:** 2 dice
- **Biotech augments**
- **Trait: Natural** – You pass as natural organic human and any biotech augments you have are very difficult to detect with a simple scan and can only be revealed through thorough investigation, such as full bloodwork and a complete autopsy.

## Cybertech
Cybertech frame is the standard tech for most cyborgs. It is relatively affordable, quite durable and support a wide variety of augments.

- **Soak:** 3 dice
- **Cybertech augments**

### Synthetic
Synthetic frames are a sub-category of cybertech. They can use cybertech augments and are very similar to cybertech frames, except human consciousness is unable to withstand a synthetic frame for long periods of time, the sensation of remoteness makes individuals unstables. Synthetic frames are however common with **Androids**.

- **Soak:** 4 dice
- **Cybertech augments**
- **Trait: Efficient** – [[energy|Energy Resource Checks]] only fail on a result of 1.
- **Trait: Uncanny valley** – Take -1 penalty on all social tests.
- **Trait: Unstable** – [[stress|Stress Check]] fails if any dice show 1-3.

## Nanotech

4th generation augmentation technology is based on microscopic machines *nanites* injected to the bloodstream. The nanites can enhance the frame’s durability and allows utilizing nanotech-based augments. Nanotech frames are so far only used by [[section-12|Section 12]].

- **Soak**: 2 dice
- **Nanotech Augments**
- **Trait: Natural** – You pass as natural organic human and any biotech augments you have are very difficult to detect with a simple scan and can only be revealed through thorough investigation, such as full bloodwork and a complete autopsy.
- **Trait: Nanite Dependency** – Nanotech frame depends on a horde of nanites in the bloodstream, and in fact vital functions may stop if insufficient nanites are present. Likewise normal medical procedures are ineffective and the frame can only benefit effectively from nanite-based healing methods or specialized surgical units.
