
Biomorphs can be used to mutate the frame in a rapid rate to grow extra layers of protection from muscle, bones, tendons, or use Biomorphs to transform your flesh into deadly weapons of flesh, bone and tendons. You can increase your mass and density, forming new growth and matter. The process is almost instantaneous and by the time you see the transformation, it's likely too late. 

The process is generally called creating a Biomorph Mutation. Mutation is always permanent unless recalled. Mutating the frame and then recalling it takes just a **short** duration. The sight of a mutated *abomination* is so gruesome most characters witnessing it will take [[stress]].
