> _“Combat is chaos. TAC-COM makes it a symphony — and you are the conductor.”_

**TAC-COM** _(Tactical Control-Command Interface)_ is a real-time battlefield coordination suite that syncs the cyborg’s mind with allied systems through encrypted command pulses, predictive modeling, and rapid-response overlays. It doesn’t just enhance team communication — it lets the user sculpt the flow of battle in ways others can’t.

TAC-COM supercharges tactical teams, enabling them to alter the flow of combat, enhance the team’s performance and orchestrate decisive action under fire.
