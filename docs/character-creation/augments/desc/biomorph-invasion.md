
Invade a target with parasitic Biomorphs that latch onto the target's biological components. Gain access to their motor functions and even cognitive state, absorb memories and skills and even brainwash the subject. Synthetic frames cannot be invaded, but even Cybernetic frames are susceptible to invasion, although the cybernetic implants make it more difficult, as both the biomorphs and cybernetics struggle for control. Target must be within close range and unable or unwilling to resist physically. It is practical to first physically incapacitate the target before attempting invasion.
