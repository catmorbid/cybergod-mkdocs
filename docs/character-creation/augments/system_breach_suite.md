A collection of software and hardware to enable system breach via direct neural connection, integrated directly to your frame. You can replicate effects of System Breach Suite easily with external equipment, but having the tools integrated to your frame is more practical.

## Overview
- **Domain:** System Breach utility
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy to activate in an Instant once per Action.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Hacking, Troubleshooting
#### Passive Effects
 - **Breach Utility** • **Hack Reroll:** 1<br>You gain free rerolls on System Breach tests.
 - **Diagnostic Utility** • **Troubleshooting Reroll:** 1<br>You gain free rerolls when using Troubleshooting for diagnostic tasks and cyber defense.
 - **Cyber Armor** • **Hard Armor (Cyber Damage):** 2<br>Take reduced Cyberdamage.
