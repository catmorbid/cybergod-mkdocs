The HERMES Mobility Suite is an Advanced Mobility enhancement suite that augments the cyborg's movement capabilities, helping them move faster in combat and evade attacks easier.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Mobility
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Mobility (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Turn.

## Flaws
- **Risky Moves**: Mobility Tests and high-risk and carry Risky Moves -complication. On failure to overcome character taks 1d6 Harm.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Mobility
#### Active Effects
- **Evasion Surge** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Gain **4 Evasion** for **Short** duration.
- **Jump Surge** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>You can jump incredible heights (up to 4 times normal) and distances. Land safely from 20m fall.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Mobility
#### Active Effects
- **Mode: Roller Blades** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Activate Roller Blades for **Moderate** duration. **High Velocity** but take Complication for rough terrain.
- **Sprint Surge** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Ignore normal mobility and move 3 range increments for free during **Short** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Mobility
#### Active Effects
- **Mode: Jump Jets** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Free reroll on mobility tests for **Moderate** duration. Use energy for additional rerolls.
