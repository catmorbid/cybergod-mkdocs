An asymmetric warfare system that allows disruption of communications, electronics and augmentations. Designed for anti-cyborg warfare, the **Disruptor Suite** can be a powerful tool that enhances tactics and weakens powerful augment-reliant cyborgs.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Electronic Warfare and interference
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Design (Cyber)` to activate as an Action once per Scene.

## Level 1
#### Active Effects
- **Signal Cloak** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 3<br>Hide any communication signals within **Long** radius for **Moderate** duration. Prevents signal disruption. Stack indicates power when opposed with disruption.
- **Augment Disruption** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 2<br>Disrupt an random augment on **Target** in **Long** range for **Short** duration. Disrupted Augment cannot be activated. Target can **Resist** with `Troubleshooting (Cyber) Hard (3)`.

## Level 2
#### Active Effects
- **Signal Disruption** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 3<br>Block communication signals in **Long** radius for **Moderate** duration. Stack indicates power when opposed with signal cloak.
- **Signal Hijack** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 3<br>Hijack specific signal frequency for **Moderate** duration to intercept signals and communications or even inject new data in the mix. Stack indicates power when opposed with signal cloak.

## Level 3
#### Active Effects
- **Augment Trap** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 2<br>Sets up a trap that triggers when **Target** uses any aguments for **Short** duration. When triggered, inflict **DC 6** **Biofeedback**.
