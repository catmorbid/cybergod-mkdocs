---
title: Advantage Augment Effect
tags:
  - Advantage
---
- Spend **1 Energy** to gain **Advantage Die** for **Short duration** (one round or one action, whichever is longer duration), effect scales with **Augment Level**.
	- **Level 1:** D6 Advantage
	- **Level 2:** D8 Advantage
	- **Level 3:** D10 Advantage
- Does not require **Energy Check** – but affects energy usage for active augments.
- Advantage Die is added to relevant skill tests.
