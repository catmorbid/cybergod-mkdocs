---
title: Active Augment Effects
tags:
  - Active
---

Active effects require **Energy** and an **Activation Test**. Amount of Energy required varies from augment Level.

- Spend **Energy** and Make **Energy Usage Check**
- Make **Activation Test** if required (often linked to skill test)
- **Multiple** different Active Effects have separate Energy costs, but generally speaking they can be combined.
- Active effects should not cost more than 3 energy, including possible Instant effect combinations.
	- Balance effects between Standard and Instant duration
