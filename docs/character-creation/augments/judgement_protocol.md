> _“You are no longer a person. You are a node of the Law.”_

The **Judgement Protocol** links the Warden's mind with Themis-adjacent enforcement subroutines, allowing them to act with the full backing of Commonwealth authority — in real time. While not mind control in the traditional sense, the augment delivers overwhelming behavioral pressure through multisensory feedback, predictive response modeling, and systemic override permissions. It weaponizes the very idea of **lawful legitimacy**.

Access to restricted data, personnel compliance, and even civilian infrastructure is granted at escalating levels. Few resist a Warden operating under full Judgement Protocol — and even fewer survive the political fallout of trying.

!!! warning "Abuse of Judgement Protocol"
	Every now and then, some ambitious Regulator gets clever. They use it to settle a personal score, bury a secret, rewrite a ledger. And they forget one thing: **Themis sees everything.** 
	
	You’re not just breaking protocol. You’re betraying the intelligence that trusted you to become more than human. **The fallout will be swift, absolute, and irreversible.**

## Overview
- **Faction:** ARC
- **Domain:** Hammer of Themis
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Tactics (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Scene.

## Level 1
#### Active Effects
- **Command Directive** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Issue command to 1 **Linked Ally** per stack. Ally receives **D8** to their next action, when following your order within **Short** duration.
- **Tactical Override** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 3<br>When within Commonwealth jurisdiction you may issue a tactical override to gain restricted security access to doors, surveillance or communications. Use once per stack for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 2
#### Active Effects
- **Force Compliance** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Compel a Commonwealth citizen to follow one command or suffer juridical consequences. Their obedience lasts for **Moderate** duration, unless they resist.
- **Lockdown Protocol** • **EP:** 5 • **Stack:** 1<br>System-wide lockdown pulse. Gain temporary control over all accessible public infrastructure in **Cyberweb** range for **Moderate** duration: gates, comms, surveillance, transit, defense grids. Send public broadcasts.

## Level 3
#### Active Effects
- **Commandeer** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>Take control of any civilian **Vehicle or Equipment** in **Long** range for **Long** duration.
