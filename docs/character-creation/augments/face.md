FACE is an active feromone agent manipulation system that acts as a powerful social enhancer.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Social manipulation and analytics
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech, Biotech, Nanotech
- **Risk Flags:** Unsafe
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Manipulation (Cyber)` to activate as an Action once per Scene.

## Flaws
- **Addictive**: Make a safety check after each scene if augment was used

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Manipulation, Networking, Persuasion
#### Active Effects
- **Social Reroll** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 3<br>Gain 1 reroll on social tests against **Target** in **Close** range for **Moderate** duration.
- **Sway Emotion** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Neuter a specific emotional response for **Target** in **Close** range for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Manipulation, Networking, Persuasion
#### Active Effects
- **Induce Emotion** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 2<br>1 **Target** in **Close** range becomes overwhelmed by a strong emotion for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Manipulation, Networking, Persuasion
