Enter a state of constant adrenaline frenzy improving close quarters fighting ability and physical prowess.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Close Combat
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech, Biotech, Nanotech
- **Risk Flags:** Unsafe
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Toughness (Body)` to activate in an Instant once per Turn.

## Flaws
- **Adrenaline Crash**: Take a -2 penalty to Reflex tests for moderate duration after effects end

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Fighting, Mobility, Toughness
#### Active Effects
- **Pain Tolerance** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>Increase Pain Thresholds by 1 per stack for **Moderate** duration.
- **Stress Tolerance** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Increase **Stress Threshold** by **1/stack** for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Fighting, Mobility, Toughness
#### Active Effects
- **Strength Surge** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Gain **+2 DC** **Kinetic** damage in close combat for **Short** duration.
- **Speed Surge** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Gain **+1 Initiative** and **Evasion** for **Short** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Fighting, Mobility, Toughness
#### Active Effects
- **Vitality Surge** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Recover 5 Shock and 1 condition in **Instant** duration.
- **Strike Surge** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Gain 4 extra hits on one attack.
