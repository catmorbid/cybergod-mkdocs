The Sensor augment improve a character’s detection capabilities by enabling various advanced modes or perception and adding additional detection systems such as a built-in radar or sonar.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Detection and awareness
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Alertness (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Scene.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Alertness
#### Active Effects
- **Low-Light Vision** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 1<br>Ignore penalties due to poor lighting and darkness for **Moderate** duration.
- **Thermal Vision** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Detect thermal radiation through obstacles for **Moderate** duration. Enables sensing through optical cloak.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Alertness
#### Active Effects
- **X-Ray Vision** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>See through walls and obstacles to scan for hidden weapons or see through optical/thermal camouflage for **Moderate** duration.
- **Electromagnetic Vision** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Sense EM radiation for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Alertness
#### Active Effects
- **Motion Detector** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Detect movement in **Direction (180 degrees)** for up to **Long** range for **Moderate** duration.
- **Sonar** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>Sonar-based detection that enables full visibility even in pitch-black or underwater for **Long** range (range +2 when underwater) for **Moderate** duration.
