> _“You are no longer a person. You are a node of the Law.”_

The **Judgement Protocol** links the Warden's mind with Themis-adjacent enforcement subroutines, allowing them to act with the full backing of Commonwealth authority — in real time. While not mind control in the traditional sense, the augment delivers overwhelming behavioral pressure through multisensory feedback, predictive response modeling, and systemic override permissions. It weaponizes the very idea of **lawful legitimacy**.

Access to restricted data, personnel compliance, and even civilian infrastructure is granted at escalating levels. Few resist a Warden operating under full Judgement Protocol — and even fewer survive the political fallout of trying.

!!! warning "Abuse of Judgement Protocol"
	Every now and then, some ambitious Regulator gets clever. They use it to settle a personal score, bury a secret, rewrite a ledger. And they forget one thing: **Themis sees everything.** 
	
	You’re not just breaking protocol. You’re betraying the intelligence that trusted you to become more than human. **The fallout will be swift, absolute, and irreversible.**


**System**
- **Activate:** Spend 1 Energy to Activate as Instant Action. You can choose one effect from any of the effects listed up to your Augment Level. You can activate multiple effects if required, but each activation costs more energy.
- **Requires:** Faction: ARC. Warden frame or other ARC Frame at higher level.
- **Conflict:** Cannot stack with control effects provided by [[psionics]], [[cyberjacking-suite]] or [[biomorph-invasion]] on the same target
- **Linked Ally:** Any ally, team mate or temporary battle buddy; you must be able to communicate properly through wireless comms, and they must give you access to their AR feed.

### Level 1

**Command Imprint** – You gain tactical override access to Commonwealth-regulated systems when within Commonwealth Cyberweb range.

- **Directive Command:** Issue orders to up to 3 **Linked Allies**. While following these orders they receive **D6 advantage** on relevant tests.
- **Tactical Override:** Once per scene, you may issue a tactical override to:
	- Open sealed Commonwealth doors.   
	- Gain temporary access to surveillance feeds, encrypted comms, or secure vehicles.    
	- Force a Commonwealth citizen or subordinate to assist you. You have **D10 advantage** on social tests against them if they resist.

### Level 2

**Operational Authority** – Your influence extends across combat infrastructure, systems of control, and civilian administration.

- **Expanded Command:** You may now issue **Directive Commands** to up to **5** **Linked Allies**. They gain **D8 advantage** while following orders, and do not suffer from **Mental Breakdown**, and are immune to **Suppression** while under your command link.
- **Force Compliance:** Once per scene, you may target a hostile or neutral actor within Cyberweb range. Test `Manipulation + Cyber or Mind` with **D10 Advantage** against the target’s `Toughness + Mind or Cyber`.  On success, the target is compelled to follow a single command. They’ll act against their better judgment, rationalizing it afterward —  
	_“Well… they did just threaten me with a 90% generational income tax.”_
- **Asset Commandeering:** You may take immediate control of:
    - **A vehicle, drone, or system** (e.g., defense turrets, public announcements, power grid) by using Commonwealth backdoor access. Jury Rigged and extra secure systems are not affected, but any public domain or civilian vehicles and systems are.
    - **A secure terminal or node**, allowing you to access private files, redirect funds, unseal classified data, or shut down accounts.
      Use of commandeering must be within a jurisdiction under active Commonwealth influence and may trigger consequences if logged improperly.

### Level 3

**Executor Node** – You are recognized by the system — and often by people — as a living instrument of Commonwealth law.

- **Protocol Lockdown:** Once per session, you may activate a **system-wide lockdown pulse**. For the next few minutes:    
    - **All Commonwealth-aligned personnel** within Cyberweb range are compelled to obey or become destabilized. Most NPCs automatically comply unless protected by rank or firewalls.        
    - You gain **temporary control** over all accessible public infrastructure in range: gates, comms, surveillance, transit, defense grids.
- **Unlimited Command:** You may now issue **Directive Commands** to any number of networked allies within range. They receive a **D10 Advantage** on related tests, and may **Reroll** any one test until end of scene.
- **Condemn Target:** Once per scene, you may **mark a single enemy as Condemned**. Test `Manipulation + Cyber or Mind` with a **D12 advantage** against their `Toughness + Mind or Cyber`
    - On success, they must comply with any commands you give them, or suffer a [[stress#Mental Breakdown|Mental Breakdown]] every time they resist (GM can also choose a suitable result for narrative emphasis). Condemnation lasts until you grant them mercy. 
    - On failure, the target resists and become immune to your attempts to **Force Compliance** or **Compel** them, and becomes aware of your presence, gaining a trace on your location. It is possible for them to receive access to some sensitive intel through an exposed two-way connection (narrative complication, subject to GM).
    - **Maintain:** Effect must be maintained by spending 1 energy per turn (or scene, when out of combat).
