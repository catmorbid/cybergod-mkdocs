Adjustable Nanite-based combat suite that can be used to graft armor and weaponry to the nanotech frame. Ultra tier Nano Forge can even craft complex machinery.

**System**
- Burn 1 energy to create graft.
- Grafting takes Short moment to finish.
- Grafts are permanent attachments to frame.
- Extreme energy usage - each graft adds a Burn (no energy checks needed). Burn is peristent and only fades when graft is deactivated.
- Grafts can be changed to another type of graft easily – burn remains the same, but the shape of graft changes.
- L1 – Medium Armor, melee weapons
- L2 – Heavy Armor, simple projectile weapons, exoskeleton assisted mobility
- L3 – Ultra Armor, complex weapons
