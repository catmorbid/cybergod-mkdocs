A modified ocular system which has an array of multiple *Ocular Drones* that can be launched independently to provide visual coverage of a large area. The image of all the drones is combined in real-time to a visual matrix combined from the image of all the drones.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Observation, Espionage
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Alertness (Cyber)` to activate as an Action once per Scene.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Alertness
#### Active Effects
- **Eyes in the Sky** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>Observe an area of 100x100m within per stack within **Extreme** range for **Moderate** duration.
- **Tracker** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>Mark and automatically track 1 target/stack for **Moderate** duration with Eyes in the Sky.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Alertness
#### Active Effects
- **Longshot** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>You can ignore range penalties for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Alertness
