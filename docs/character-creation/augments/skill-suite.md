A complex augment affecting the brain and neural system that can override neural connections injecting simulated skills and knowledge via muscle memory and memory implants.

**System - draft**
- Activate with 1 Energy. Duration full scene.
- L1
	- Choose 2 skills, write them down. When augment is active, those skills are used at Professional level.
- L2
	- You can choose total 3 skills: 2 Professional, 1 Expert.
	- One of your skills is a Wild Card, and you don’t need to determine it beforehand, and can instead choose it when the time is right.
- L3
	- You can choose total 4 skills: 2 Professional, 1 Expert and 1 Master.
	- Two of your skills can be a Wild Card.
