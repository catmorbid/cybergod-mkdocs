Takes a backup of consciousness in realtime. Provided you have the means to deploy a new frame, you could be back in action within minutes of a Frame Death incident. 

!!! info Passive energy draw
	Immortal is always active and it **permanently** reduces available Energy by an amount equal to Augment Level. You can choose to disable it, but do you really want to?

## Overview
- **Domain:** Remote QDC Backup
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech, Nanotech
- **Activation:** Always active.

## Level 1
#### Passive Effects
 - **Remote Backup** • **Restore Backup:** Once every 2 game sessions<br>You are backed up to your Soul Server realtime. Augment Level determines how often a backup can be restored.

## Level 2
#### Passive Effects
 - **Remote Backup** • **Restore Backup:** Once every game session<br>You are backed up to your Soul Server realtime. Augment Level determines how often a backup can be restored.

## Level 3
#### Passive Effects
 - **Remote Backup** • **Restore Backup:** Twice per game session<br>You are backed up to your Soul Server realtime. Augment Level determines how often a backup can be restored.
