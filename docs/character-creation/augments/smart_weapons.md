You can interface with Smart Weapons and ordnance and use guided projectiles and access their combat data. **Smart Weapons** augment is ideal for high rate of fire weaponry enabling high number of Hits on single - and even multiple targets - compared to regular weapons.

Any Personal Weapons are automatically modified to be compatible, but random weapons picked from dead enemies are likely incompatible. Even if they were, Smart Weapons have high security and need to be hacked before they can be used.

!!! warning "Compatibility and Smart Tag"
	Smart Weapons are only compatible with conventional firearms and slow projectile launches, such as missile launchers or grenade launchers and cannot be used with Gauss weapons, Shock Weapons, Sonic Weapons, Laser weapons or Plasma weapons. Any weapons that are modified for Smart Weapons augment should have the **Smart** tag added. This will reduce their damage in exchange for other benefits. See **System** for details.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Guided projectiles
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Aim (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Action.

## Flaws
- **SMART Mods**: Requires all weapons to be modified compatible with SMART Mods. Personal weapons are automatically modified.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Networked Fire Control** • **Indirect Fire penalty:** -2<br>You can attack any target with SMART weapons detected by yourself or allies with some indirect fire penalty.

#### Active Effects
- **Trigger Control** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 2<br>Reduce ammo cost by 1 for **Short** duration.
- **Hit Stack** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Gain 1 additional Hit from all Hit Effects

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Networked Fire Control** • **Indirect Fire penalty:** -1<br>You can attack any target with SMART weapons detected by yourself or allies with some indirect fire penalty.

#### Active Effects
- **Bypass Cover** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 1<br>Ignore Cover for the attack.
- **Telemetry** • **EP:** 1<br>Receive telemetry data from projectiles.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Networked Fire Control** • **Indirect Fire penalty:** No Penalty<br>You can attack any target with SMART weapons detected by yourself or allies with some indirect fire penalty.

#### Active Effects
- **Multi Target** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 3<br>Each Hit effect hits an additional target.
- **Drone Bullet** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Remote-control a single SMART projectile for **Moderate** duration. Make a **Pilot (Reflex)** test to control a bullet to its target, ignoring range.
