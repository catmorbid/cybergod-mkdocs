Specialized Tactical Marksmanship enhancement is an ideal augment for fire-team leaders and sniper spotters. **Pinpoint** automatically tracks marked targets and provides a real-time feed to allies on the targets and their positions. The system can also identify vulnerabilities, improving attack effectiveness against the targets.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Augmented Marksmanship Support
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Tactics (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Turn.

## Level 1
#### Active Effects
- **Mark Target** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>Mark a target within **Long** range for anyone networked with you for **Moderate** duration. Attacks against target have **D8** **Advantage**.
- **Expose Vulnerability** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Next attack against target by you or ally within **Short** duration inflicts double damage.

## Level 2
#### Active Effects
- **Expose Weakness** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Next attack against target has **+2** **Armor Piercing**
