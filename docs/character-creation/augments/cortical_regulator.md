> _“Fear is a chemical reaction; doubt is an electrical impulse. Both can be bypassed.”_  
> —Excerpt from the R&D notes of Neural Governor, GenSys Corp.

A specialized neural implant or bio-mod that suppresses emotional surges—both positive and negative—and enables the user to maintain a cold, calculating state of mind even under extreme stress. At higher levels, the implant can forcibly override deep-rooted inhibitions, accelerate cognitive processing, and impose partial mental “lockdowns.”

## Overview
- **Domain:** Combat, survival
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech, Biotech, Nanotech
- **Risk Flags:** Unsafe
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Toughness (Mind)` to activate in an Instant once per Scene.

## Flaws
- **Suppressed Empathy**: When augment is activated, take -3 penalty to any Empathic tests.
- **Emotional Crash**: Take a -2 penalty to Mind tests for moderation duration after ending effects

## Level 1
#### Active Effects
- **Stress Tolerance** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Increase **Stress Threshold** by **1/stack** for **Moderate** duration.
- **Focus** • **EP:** 4 • **Stack:** 1<br>Gain 1 reroll on every Mind-based tests for **Moderate** duration.

## Level 2
#### Active Effects
- **Stress Lockdown** • **EP:** 4 • **Stack:** 1<br>Suspend any stress checks for **Moderate** duration. Make stress check immediately after effect ends.
- **Force Override** • **EP:** 4 • **Stack:** 1<br>One **Target** in **Touch** range becomes completely emotionally suppressed and unresponsive to fear, empathy or compassion for **Moderate** duration. It can break them from psionic or mind control influence, but may also cause unpredictable side-effects, subject to GM discretion. Equally useful for interrogating a subject or preventing someone from lashing out.

## Level 3
#### Active Effects
- **Mental Fortress** • **EP:** 5 • **Stack:** 1<br>Become immune to mind manipulation, suggestions and stress for **Moderate** duration.
