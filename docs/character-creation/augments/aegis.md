Create energy field to protect yourself and those nearby. The shield lasts for a **Short** duration, but it can be maintained by spending another point of Energy.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Defense and Protection
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Risk Flags:** High-Energy
- **Activation:** Spend 2 Energy and test `Toughness (Cyber)` to activate as an Action once per Turn.

## Flaws
- **Critical Burn**: Whenever you fail a Burn Check and Aegis was activated, you take 1d6 Harm.

## Level 1
#### Active Effects
- **Deflect** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 5<br>Barrier can deflect hits, adding 1 Evasion per stack for **Moderate** duration.
- **Energy Barrier** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>120 degree barrier that can negate 1 **Energy** Hit per stack for **Short** duration.

## Level 2
#### Active Effects
- **Ballistic Barrier** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>120 degree barrier that can negate 1 **Ballistic** Hit per stack for **Short** duration.
- **Enlarged Barrier** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 2<br>Improve the area covered by the barrier. Barrier can protect **Self** and all targets in **Close** radius +1 per additional stack; or you can increase it to cover additional 120 degrees in one direction per stack, up to full a full circle at 360 degrees.

## Level 3
#### Active Effects
- **Kinetic Barrier** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 5<br>120 degree barrier that can negate 1 **Kinetic** Hit per stack for **Short** duration.
- **Durable Barrier** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 2<br>Barrier can withstand twice as many hits.
