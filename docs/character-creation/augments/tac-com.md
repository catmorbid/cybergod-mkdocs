> _“Combat is chaos. TAC-COM makes it a symphony — and you are the conductor.”_

**TAC-COM** _(Tactical Control-Command Interface)_ is a real-time battlefield coordination suite that syncs the cyborg’s mind with allied systems through encrypted command pulses, predictive modeling, and rapid-response overlays. It doesn’t just enhance team communication — it lets the user sculpt the flow of battle in ways others can’t.

TAC-COM supercharges tactical teams, enabling them to alter the flow of combat, enhance the team’s performance and orchestrate decisive action under fire.

**System**
- **Activate:** Spend 1 Energy as an Instant Action. You may choose one effect from any level you have access to. You may activate multiple effects with additional Energy
- **Augmented Tactics:** Any `Tactics` skill tests can be made using either `Cyber` or `Mind` as the attribute.
- **Linked Ally:** Any ally, team mate or temporary battle buddy; you must be able to communicate properly through wireless comms, and they must give you access to their AR feed.

### Level 1

**Command Sync** – Establishes a temporary command link with allies in direct communication range.

- **Tactical Advantage:** You gain **D6 Advantage** on all `Tactics` tests.
- **Linked Reaction:** Once per round, you may designate a **Linked Ally**: When they succeed on an action, you may immediately follow up with a **Free Reaction** that relates to or is supported by your Ally’s action.
- **Attack by Fire:** Once per scene, you may declare a **Pre-Built Maneuver** (e.g. pincer, breach stack, fallback). All allies involved receive **D10 Advantage** when executing it. The bonus applies even if split across multiple turns. You must plan the maneuver ahead, and GM has to approve the conditions are right.

### Level 2

**Tempo Override** – You can now dynamically alter the flow of combat for tactical advantage.

- **Improved Tactical Advantage:** You gain **D8 Advantage** on `Tactics` tests.
- **Command Rush:** Once per round, as an Instant Action, you may give a **Linked Ally** an additional Action *immediately after your action*, even if they’ve already acted. This costs 1 additional Energy, and your *Linked Ally* takes 1 Shock due to physical strain.
- **Command Chain:** Designate up to 3 Linked Allies:  
    - They receive **D8 Advantage** on their next test as long as the test relates to a **shared objective** (e.g., suppressing same target, protecting asset, regrouping).

### Level 3

**Full Synchronization** – You’re not just coordinating actions — you are shaping outcomes before they happen.

- **Superior Tactical Advantage:** You gain **D10 Advantage** on `Tactics` tests.    
- **Strategic Dominance**: Activate once per scene, lasts for 2 rounds:
    - All **Linked Allies** gain a free [[skill-test-v4#Rerolls|Reroll]] each round
    - You may **force one enemy** per round to automatically lose **Initiative**, when relevant.
- **Zero-Time Maneuver:** Declare a synchronized action (e.g. triple shot, breach-clear-suppress, timed retreat) with up to 4 Linked Allies. All act simultaneously. Treat results as if they happened at once; all actions are resolved before any enemies can react and any damage dealt to shared targets are accumulated before checking their [[stats#Damage Threshold|Damage Threshold]].
