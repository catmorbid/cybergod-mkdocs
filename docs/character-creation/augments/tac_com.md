> _“Combat is chaos. TAC-COM makes it a symphony — and you are the conductor.”_

**TAC-COM** _(Tactical Control-Command Interface)_ is a real-time battlefield coordination suite that syncs the cyborg’s mind with allied systems through encrypted command pulses, predictive modeling, and rapid-response overlays. It doesn’t just enhance team communication — it lets the user sculpt the flow of battle in ways others can’t.

TAC-COM supercharges tactical teams, enabling them to alter the flow of combat, enhance the team’s performance and orchestrate decisive action under fire.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Tactical Enhancement
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Tactics (Cyber/Mind)` to activate in an Instant once per Scene.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Tactics
#### Active Effects
- **Linked Reaction** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Once within **Short** duration, if **Linked Ally** succeeds in an action, you can follow-up with a Free Reaction that supports their action.
- **Attack by Fire** • **EP:** 3 • **Stack:** 1<br>A maneuver planned ahead, you can use once per scene. **Team** in **Network** range get **D12** modifier when executing the maneuver within **Short** duration.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Tactics
#### Active Effects
- **Action Surge (linked)** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 4<br>1 **Linked Ally** in **Network** range gains 1 Action for **Short** duration.
- **Command Chain** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>1 **Linked Ally** per stack receives **D8** modifier to their next test with shared objective within **Short** duration.

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Tactics
#### Active Effects
- **Strategic Dominance** • **EP:** 5 • **Stack:** 2<br>**Linked Allies** within **Network** range gain a **Free Reroll** for **Short** duration.
- **Zero-Time Maneuver** • **EP:** 5 • **Stack:** 1<br>Declare a synchronized action (e.g. triple shot, breach-clear-suppress, timed retreat) with **4 Linked Allies** in **Network** range that must be executed in **Moderate** duration. Actions are synchronized and results apply simultaneously.
