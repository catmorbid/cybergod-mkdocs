Enables extremely high damage and precision in combat by extreme calculation power, which is displayed as slowed down time and trajectory tracking. Effective in both close combat and ranged combat.

## Overview
- **Domain:** Damage and Precision enhancement
- **Tech Profile:** Cybertech
- **Activation:** Spend 1 Energy and test `Aim (Cyber) / Fighting (Cyber)` to activate in an Instant once per Action.

## Level 1
- **Advantage:** D6 to Fighting, Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Combat Awareness**<br>Gain one free reroll on attack test.
 - **Motion Vector Compensation** • **Velocity or Evasion mitigation:** Ignore up to -1 penalty<br>Compensate for Velocity or Evasion when making ranged attacks.

#### Active Effects
- **Snap Hit** • **EP:** 1 • **Stack:** 3<br>Inflict **1 Hit** to **Target** within **Moderate** range with **+2 initiative** using any Fire Mode, but normal Hit Limits apply.
- **Aim Bot** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 1<br>Inflict any **Aimed Hit** -effect.

## Level 2
- **Advantage:** D8 to Fighting, Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Combat Awareness**<br>Gain one free reroll on attack test.
 - **Motion Vector Compensation** • **Velocity or Evasion mitigation:** Ignore up to -2 penalty<br>Compensate for Velocity or Evasion when making ranged attacks.

#### Active Effects
- **Trick Shot** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 5<br>Upgrade a Hit. Ricochet the projectile at an angle to hit a target normally not in line of fire, to e.g. ignore a cover. Stack for more complex shots. **Laser** weapons can ricochet if a reflective surface is available, and **Plasma** and **Gauss** weapons can only ricochet if a powerful force field (such as [[aegis|Aegis]] augment) is nearby.
- **Nerve Point** • **EP:** 2 • **Stack:** 2<br>Close Combat only. Upgrade a Hit to inflict **+2** **Damage** and **Armor Piercing**

## Level 3
- **Advantage:** D10 to Fighting, Aim
#### Passive Effects
 - **Combat Awareness**<br>Gain one free reroll on attack test.
 - **Motion Vector Compensation** • **Velocity or Evasion mitigation:** Ignore up to -3 penalty<br>Compensate for Velocity or Evasion when making ranged attacks.
