Many kinds of drugs exists. Some are legal and approved for official use, while others are strictly illegal. The black market thrives on illegal drugs.

### Safety Check
You must make a [[engrams#Safety Check|Safety Check]] whenever you use any drugs using the indicated Safety Die. Over-use may lead to [[engrams#Corruption|Corruption]] and loss of humanity. Safety Checks can be re-rolled by spending a point of [[resolve|Resolve]], which might be a good idea to avoid taking corruption.
