Each character can choose one Frame Template from those provided by their Faction. This is their baseline and they can then spend [[upgrades|Upgrade]] their template further.

Frame Templates are mass-produced frames that can easily fit modular augments. Most Frames are cybernetically enhanced **Bioframes**, grown in a vat, designed with clear specifications in mind. Individual Frames are mixed with the intended owner’s genetic markers, which should help with compatibility and will also retain the character’s original appearance. 

## Natural Frames

Each character was born at some point into a natural frame. This natural frame is usually stored in cryogenic storage and most characters won’t see much time in their original body. Some Factions may provide a low-grade **Bioframe** that shares the character’s natural frame’s genome, but without costly augments. This can be used as alternative to their augmented primary Frame.

A natural Frame has D6 in all Attributes, but if you take one Attribute at D4, you may take one at D8. A faction-provided Natural Frame has a Cybernetic Brain, and can be backed up, but your original frame does not have one, nor can it have - non-enhanced natural frames have high rejection rate to cybernetics, and simply will not work with a Cybernetic Brain.

## Faction Frame Templates
Each [[factions/index|Faction]] has its own set of Frame Templates. Characters must choose one of the templates. They can customize the frames to some extent, but there are limitations.

You can read more about Frame Templates on each template’s page. Use the augment list to pick your upgrades and optionally you can utilize the pre-made **Upgrade Path** table.
